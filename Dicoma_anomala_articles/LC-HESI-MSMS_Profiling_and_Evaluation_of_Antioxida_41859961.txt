Abstract:
This study aims to assess the biological characteristics of Rhus tripartitum leaves and identify their promising biomolecules. The numerous extracts of R. tripartitum leaves were evaluated, in vitro, for their phytochemical profile and antioxidant, anti-acetylcholinesterase, α-amylase, and anti-inflammatory activities. The methanolic and ethyl acetate extracts showed the highest levels of phenolics, flavonoids, and tannins, and exhibited the strongest antioxidant effects in DPPH, FRAP, and TAC assays.
