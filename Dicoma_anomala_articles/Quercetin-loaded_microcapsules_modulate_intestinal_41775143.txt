Title: Quercetin-loaded microcapsules modulate intestinal serotonin, cell proliferation, and mucosa architecture in adjuvant-induced arthritis.

Authors: Cicero, Lídia Rodrigues; Bossolani, Gleison Daion Piovezana; Kuller, João Victor; Bersani-Amado, Ciomar Aparecida; Verri, Waldiceu Aparecido; Godoy, Marcos Yudi Nagaoka; Sanches, Mariana Rodrigues; Perles, Juliana Vanessa Colombo Martins; Zanoni, Jacqueline Nelisis

Year: 2026

PMID: 41775143

Keywords/MeSH: Antioxidant; Inflammation; Quercetin; Rheumatoid arthritis; Small intestine

Publication Types: Journal Article

Abstract:
We investigated whether the effect of treatment with quercetin-loaded microcapsules 10 mg/kg promotes anti-inflammatory action and prevents damage to the intestinal wall in the jejunum of rats with adjuvant-induced arthritis. Thirty Holtzman rats were distributed into 5 groups: C (control), control administered with quercetin-loaded microcapsules 10 mg/kg (CQ), adjuvant-induced arthritis (AIA), adjuvant-induced arthritis administered with ibuprofen 17.5 mg/kg (AI) and adjuvant-induced arthritis administered with quercetin-loaded microcapsules 10 mg/kg (AQ).
